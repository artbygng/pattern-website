A tiny crocheted pot with a lid, three little handles, and a happy face — a quick, satisfying amigurumi make and completely free. This pattern is worked in continuous rounds and written in US crochet terms.

**Notes:**
- This pattern is worked in continuous rounds.
- Stitch totals are shown in **[square]** brackets at the end of each round.
- Repeating instructions appear in **(round)** brackets followed by the number of repetitions needed.
- This pattern is written in US crochet terms.

## Materials

- Yarn in 2 colors, plus black for the mouth
- A hook to match your yarn (I used 3.5mm with Teddy's Classic yarn)
- Safety eyes (I used 0.8 mm)
- Fiberfill
- Tapestry needle

## Crochet terms used

- stitch (st)
- slip stitch (slst)
- single crochet (sc)
- reverse single crochet
- increase (inc)
- magic ring
- round (rnd)
- front loop only (FLO)
- back loop only (BLO)

## Pot body

<img src="/images/free-patterns/little-happy-pot-body-progress.jpg" alt="A crocheted purple pot body in progress, with a plastic embroidery mesh circle cut for the base" />

Rnd 1: 6 sc in a magic ring [6]<br/>
Rnd 2: 6 inc [12]<br/>
Rnd 3: (sc, inc) repeat 6 times [18]<br/>
Rnd 4: (sc, inc, sc) repeat 6 times [24]<br/>
Rnd 5: (3 sc, inc) repeat 6 times [30]<br/>
Rnd 6: (2 sc, inc, 2 sc) repeat 6 times [36]<br/>
Rnd 7: BLO 36 sc [36]<br/>
Rnd 8 - 13: 36 sc [36]

Slst to close. Fasten off leaving a long tail for sewing.

Cut a plastic embroidery sheet into a circle and place it in the bottom of the pot.

Insert the eyes between round 10 and 11 with 6 st between them.

Embroider the mouth.

<img src="/images/free-patterns/little-happy-pot-body-finished.jpg" alt="A finished purple crocheted pot body with a happy embroidered face, held in a hand" />

## Handles (make 3)

Rnd 1: 4 sc in a magic ring [4]<br/>
Rnd 2 - 7: 4 sc [4]

Weave in the yarn tail in the back loops of the last 4 st and pull to close.

Fasten off leaving a long tail for sewing.

Sew 2 of the handles opposite each other on round 12, with 2 st between the two ends of the handle.

The handles should be placed so that the face is in the middle.

<img src="/images/free-patterns/little-happy-pot-handles.jpg" alt="Three crocheted handle pieces, then two of them sewn onto the pot body on either side of the face" />

## Pot lid

Rnd 1: 6 sc in a magic ring [6]<br/>
Rnd 2: 6 inc [12]<br/>
Rnd 3: (sc, inc) repeat 6 times [18]<br/>
Rnd 4: (sc, inc, sc) repeat 6 times [24]<br/>
Rnd 5: (3 sc, inc) repeat 6 times [30]<br/>
Rnd 6: (2 sc, inc, 2 sc) repeat 6 times [36]<br/>
Rnd 7: FLO 36 sc [36]<br/>
Change to the second color<br/>
Rnd 8: 36 reverse sc [36]

Slst to close, fasten off, and tuck in the loose ends.

Sew the third handle to the lid, in the two opposite ends of row 2.

<img src="/images/free-patterns/little-happy-pot-lid-holding.jpg" alt="A hand holding a two-toned purple crocheted lid, before the handle is attached" />
<img src="/images/free-patterns/little-happy-pot-lid-pair.jpg" alt="The finished pot lid from two angles, showing the handle sewn on top" />

## Putting the pot together

Stuff the pot with fiberfill (not too tight).

Use the long tail from the pot to sew it to the loops created by round 7 of the lid.

<img src="/images/free-patterns/little-happy-pot-assembly.jpg" alt="Sewing the lid onto the stuffed pot body, and the finished purple and green Little Happy Pot amigurumi side by side" />
